# Plan 3.2: SA & Mechanic Role Testing

## Objective
Write E2E tests for the Portal to verify that Service Advisors (SA) and Mechanics have restricted, correctly-gated access to the application and their respective workflows.

## Context
- `webapp/tests/e2e/specs/portal-sa.spec.js`
- `webapp/tests/e2e/specs/portal-mechanic.spec.js`
