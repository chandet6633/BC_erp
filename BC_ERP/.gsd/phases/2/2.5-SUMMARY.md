# Summary 2.5: Report & Bug Aggregation

## Discoveries & Failures
- The `beforeEach` hook uniformly failed due to a timeout waiting for the navigation URL `**/index.html*`.

- A bug report was generated at `.gsd/phases/2/MungkhudShop-Bugs.md`.
- No isolated UI bugs could be mapped due to the overarching auth failure blocking the tests.
