# Frosted Precision

Frosted Precision is a visual language of quiet technical luxury: clear space, optical refraction, and exact alignment doing more work than decoration. The composition should feel meticulously crafted, as if every millimeter has been measured, tested, and refined by someone at the top of their field. Nothing is crowded. Nothing is ornamental unless it behaves like light passing through glass.

The material feeling is cold daylight over polished film: white, ice blue, obsidian text, and controlled spectral accents. Color appears as a calibrated signal, not a flood. Blue communicates trust and technology, violet hints at premium refraction, and deep black anchors the card with seriousness. This must look like master-level execution, the product of painstaking attention rather than a generic template.

Scale is the discipline. On a small physical object, hierarchy must be brutal in its clarity: logo, purpose, activation action, card ID. Secondary information becomes texture, chips, or subtle reference marks. Text is sparse and exact. The card should feel labored over with care, with every line ending, chip, and QR zone locked to a deliberate grid.

The hidden reference is automotive film as invisible protection. The design should not literally explain every glass panel; instead, it should imply front, side, and rear coverage through small systematic markers and a refracted vehicle presence. The audience should feel the subject before reading it.

Final expression should be a pristine manufactured object: flat-corner PVC, generous margins, crisp QR placement, and a light-frosted website-aligned surface. The result must feel like countless hours of refinement compressed into a small card.
